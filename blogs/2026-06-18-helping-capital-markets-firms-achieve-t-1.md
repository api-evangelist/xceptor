From Email to Automation: Helping Capital Markets Firms Achieve T+1 The transition to T+1 settlement is one of the most significant operational shifts the capital markets industry has faced in decades. Compressing the settlement cycle from two days to one dramatically reduces the time available for post-trade processes such as affirmations and trade confirmations.
