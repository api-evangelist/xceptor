<p>The transition to <strong>T+1 settlement</strong> marks a seismic shift in capital markets. By compressing post-trade timelines to just <strong>one business day</strong> between trade execution and settlement, there is little room for error and forces organisations to rethink how they manage operational processes. What was once a comfortable window for reconciliation and exception handling under T+2 has become a race against the clock. The question is:<strong> how do firms adapt without sacrificing accuracy or increasing risk?</strong><span>&nbsp;</span></p>
